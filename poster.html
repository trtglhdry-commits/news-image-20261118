WEDNESDAY · 18 NOVEMBER 2026 · 39 STORIES FROM AROUND THE GLOBE
Three leaders converge on Shenzhen, fire is exchanged across the Gulf, and the biggest IPO in history prices on Wall Street.
LEAD · SHENZHEN

Three leaders, one table in Shenzhen

APEC opened in Shenzhen as Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin held talks on Wednesday — their fifth meeting this year. Donald Trump flew in for Thursday’s first-ever trilateral summit of the three, with nuclear arms, Ukraine, Iran, trade and AI on the agenda.

LEAD · THE GULF

Fire exchanged across the Gulf

U.S. jets struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz. Iran answered with drones at U.S. sites at Kuwait’s Ali Al Salem base and at the radar at Bahrain’s Sheikh Isa base; no damage was reported. A third U.S. carrier, the USS Theodore Roosevelt, has reached the region.

LEAD · WALL STREET

The biggest IPO in history

Anthropic priced its shares at $83, raising $81.3 billion — the largest stock-market debut on record, valuing the maker of Claude at about $1.5 trillion. Trading begins Thursday on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, the first frontier AI lab to go public.

Europe

Four killed as Russia strikes Odesa

Russian strikes on the historic centre of Odesa kill four people and injure at least 13, the city’s military administration says.

EU proposes €45bn loan for Ukraine

Brussels proposes a €45 billion loan to cover Ukraine’s 2027 funding gap as the war grinds on.

Donbas-for-NATO idea reported

Zelenskyy says U.S. envoys raised Ukraine ceding Donbas in exchange for a NATO path; he calls the talks “not easy”.

France denies troops in Ukraine

Paris rejects Moscow’s claim that French troops are deployed in the war, calling it false propaganda.

Spain lifts minimum wage 5.3%

Madrid will raise the monthly minimum wage to €1,286, with a general election set for 29 November.

Middle East

Israeli troops push into central Gaza

A rare ground operation moves past the ceasefire line near Nuseirat; families flee tents as Hamas calls it a serious breach.

Peace board warns truce may collapse

Trump’s Board of Peace criticises both Israel and Hamas as the Gaza ceasefire stalls.

Iran bars nuclear inspectors

Tehran refuses IAEA inspection of its damaged nuclear sites until the Middle East war ends for good.

Houthis advance in Yemen

Houthi fighters seize ground 100 km west of Aden as Saudi-backed forces launch a counter-offensive.

Africa

Nigeria: U.S. intel hunts militants

Abuja says real-time American intelligence and near-daily missions are helping target militants ahead of the 2027 vote.

20 abducted freed in Zamfara

Police in north-west Nigeria free 20 abducted women and children, 19 of them held for 22 days.

South Africa probes 17 shooting deaths

Police continue inquiries into two weekend shootings in Pretoria and KwaZulu-Natal that left 17 dead.

Asia-Pacific

APEC opens in Shenzhen

China hosts the 21-member leaders’ meeting, with trade, AI and supply chains at the top of the agenda.

QUAD unveils new Indo-Pacific plan

In Manila, the four leaders agree joint maritime surveillance, a 2027 patrol-aircraft exercise and an AI Accelerator.

Modi offers free e-visas to ASEAN

India’s PM proposes free e-visas for ASEAN tourists at the East Asia Summit and a long-term maritime plan.

ASEAN chair passes to Singapore

Marcos hands the chair to Singapore; over 100 outcome documents are adopted and the digital economy pact is done.

Canada and China extend trade deals

Xi meets PM Mark Carney ahead of APEC as Ottawa diversifies away from dependence on the U.S. market.

Japan scrambles jets at Russian planes

Two suspected Russian military aircraft violate Japanese airspace; Taiwan also reports two Russian aircraft nearby.

Cyclone Akvan bears down on Andhra

The storm is set to cross the Andhra coast around noon on 20 November with winds of 120–130 km/h, gusting to 145.

Americas

Trump hails “very big” Philippines deal

In Manila, the U.S. president says Washington has concluded a major trade deal with the Philippines.

Fed minutes point to more hikes

Minutes show nearly all Fed officials expect further rate rises may be needed to tame inflation; the dollar hits a 19-month high.

27½ years for plot to kill Trump

A New York court jails Asif Merchant over an Iran-linked plot to assassinate Donald Trump and other U.S. leaders.

Blast at Texas chemical plant

An explosion at a hazardous-waste plant in La Porte, near Houston, shakes buildings for miles and injures one worker.

Democrats elect their leaders

U.S. Democrats hold leadership elections in Congress as the party regroups after the midterms.

Economy & Markets

Nvidia doubles sales — shares fall

Revenue hits $115.8bn, up 103% on the year, yet the stock drops 2.9% on succession doubts and the China chip halt.

Wall Street ends mixed

The S&P 500 slips 0.23% for a fifth straight fall; the Dow drops 1.11% while the Nasdaq gains 0.42%.

Gold plunges 3.5%

Gold falls to $3,728.60 an ounce and oil slides about 3% to near $82 as the dollar strengthens.

Health insurers sink on audit plan

UnitedHealth falls 10.2% and Humana 13% after a Medicare proposal to expand audits of plan payments.

Climate & Science

COP31: “lean in and move faster”

Climate leaders in Antalya urge quicker action as a deal at the summit remains elusive.

40 satellites lost in rocket failure

After the ULA Vulcan rocket fails, U.S. agencies review launch safety; Amazon’s Leo internet plan faces delay.

India’s first green jet-fuel plant ready

A Panipat refinery unit will turn used cooking oil into sustainable aviation fuel, cutting emissions by up to 80%.

Sport & Culture

Dhoni ends his IPL playing career

MS Dhoni, 45, retires as a player after five IPL titles with Chennai and becomes the team’s mentor for 2027.

India beat Pakistan 2–1 for SAFF crown

India win a record-extending 10th SAFF Championship title, with Sandip Mandi’s 88th-minute winner.

Portugal and Norway advance

Portugal beat Wales 3–2; Norway come back from 2–0 down to beat Denmark 3–2, Haaland scoring a 96th-minute penalty.

GTA VI out tomorrow after 13 years

Rockstar’s Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday; schools and bosses brace for a wave of sick days.

Van Gogh sells for record $190.4m

“Chataigniers en Fleurs” fetches a record price at auction, the top lot of the autumn sales.
